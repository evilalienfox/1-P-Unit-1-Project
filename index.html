Harajuku Fashion:

Harajuku fashion is a diverse, boundary-pushing umbrella term for Japanese street fashion styles originating in the Harajuku neighborhood of Tokyo (between Shinjuku and Shibuya), rooted in youth rebellion against strict social conformity.


Lolita is a Japanese street style subculture focused on modesty, cuteness, and a doll-like silhouette inspired largely by Victorian and Rococo clothing.
